Our Services

1. Spin Class

Our spin studio is built for members who want cardio that does not feel like a chore. Every ride is coach-led, music-driven, and designed to push your pace without wrecking your joints — the signature session for anyone chasing endurance without the pounding of the pavement.

Whether you are a beginner or an experienced individual, our sessions will challenge you to push past your limits, enhance performance develop lasting strength that empowers every aspect of your life.

Benefits:

Protects your knees and joints,builds cardiovascular endurance fast and the group setting keeps motivation and intensity high even on your toughest days.

2. Pilates and Steps

Pilates and steps at Lennix Gym builds the foundation every other workout depends on — core strength, posture, and control. It is the counterbalance to high-intensity training, giving your body the stability and mobility it needs to lift heavier and move better everywhere else.

Benefits:

Strengthens deep core muscles often missed by regular weight training, improves posture, balance and joint stabilipty, and is low-impact enough for injury recovery or active rest days — while building the mobility that makes every other workout safer and more effective. guidance.

4. Boxing

Boxing at Lennix Gym combines real technique with a serious conditioning workout — no boxing background required. Coached sessions teach genuine skill while delivering one of the most complete full-body workouts on our schedule, making it a favorite for stress relief and strength in equal measure.

Benefits:

Builds full-body strength, speed and coordination while torching calories through constant movement. It is a proven stress-reliever, sharpens reflexes and mental focus, and builds real self-defense skill — all while delivering one of the toughest cardio workouts on the floor.
